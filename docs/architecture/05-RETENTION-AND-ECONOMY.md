# MindShift: Retention & Economy Architecture

Based on the Crystal Economy Laws from VOLAURA.

## 1. Tamagotchi Streak (Loss Aversion)
- **Mechanic:** If a child doesn't log in and do a daily quest, the Pet's `mood` drops by 25 points (Max 100).
- **Rule Enforcement (Shame-Free):** The UI never says "You lost your streak". The pet says: "I missed our lessons!".
- **Streak Freeze:** Kids can buy a "Vacation Day" for the pet for 500 Crystals to freeze the mood drop.

## 2. Gacha Calendar (7-Day Login)
- **Variable Drops:** Days 1-6 drop random rewards (10 crystals, 50 crystals, or a 15% chance at a Skin Shard).
- **Fixed Drops:** Day 7 guarantees a Rare item or a completed Skin.
- **Rule Enforcement (Unexpected > Expected):** The daily reward drops automatically when the child logs in, creating a slot-machine-like surprise effect, rather than a contingent "Do homework to get 10 crystals" effect.

## 3. The Crystal Economy
- **Earn:** Daily Logins, completing Lessons, finding Easter eggs in the chat.
- **Spend:** Buying cosmetic skins for the Monster, unlocking new TTS voices, buying Streak Freezes.
