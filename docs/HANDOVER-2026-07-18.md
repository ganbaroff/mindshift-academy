## 2. What the product is

A 5-lesson, invite-only, COPPA-gated web app that teaches children (8–14) prompt engineering by
letting them raise and train a safe AI monster. Parent gives consent → child hatches a monster,
teaches it to speak, builds a cipher, corrects its "vision", and solves a maze with IF/THEN
logic. Parents get a weekly progress report and can revoke consent at any time.

Full product/architecture detail: [architecture/](architecture/) (7 briefs) and the README.

## 5. Core safety invariant (what makes this shippable to kids)

**No child free-text leaves the app before verified parental consent** — not to the generative
model, not to the safety classifier.

- `hasValidConsent()` is fail-closed and gates every child-data path server-side (chat, monster,
  tts) plus the lesson/onboarding layouts.
- The public landing preview (`/api/generate-silhouette`) is deterministic — no external call at
  all. A test statically proves the route imports no AI/moderation module.
- Two independent classifiers (Llama-Guard + kid-net) moderate child input AND AI output, and
  fail **closed** on any error. Consent copy discloses both external processors.

## 8. Risks

- **Legal (highest):** shipping to real under-13 users before A is a COPPA exposure. The code is
  correct; the legal wrapper is not signed. Keep it closed-test until A.
- **Cost:** the chat path makes ~6 LLM calls per turn. Rate limits are in place and fail-closed,
  but watch the NVIDIA/Gemini billing on first real traffic.
- **Provider dependency:** Gemini + NVIDIA are required for chat/safety; the app fails closed
  (blocks) rather than open if they're down — safe, but the child sees a "try again".
